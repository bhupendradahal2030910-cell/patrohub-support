  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Some features may require information you provide, such as birth details, place, and timezone, to generate requested results.
  • The app may display ads provided by Google AdMob.

5. Camera Permission

Patro Hub may request camera permission to scan recharge card PINs using the recharge scanner feature.

  • Camera access is used only when you choose to use the recharge scanner.
  • Scanning is performed locally on your device.
  • We do not intentionally store or upload photographs or videos captured for recharge PIN scanning.
  • The camera is not used for background recording.
  • You can deny camera permission and continue using other Patro Hub features.

6. Panchang, Moon Sign, Kundali, Astrology, and Matching

If you use Panchang, chandra rashi (moon sign), kundali, astrology, or matching-related features, the app may process information you provide, such as date, time, place, and timezone, to generate the requested result.

  • This information is processed to provide the feature you requested.
  • We do not sell this information.
  • Account login may be required if you choose to save kundali data or access saved information across devices.
  • Some calculation inputs may be processed temporarily on our servers or service infrastructure to generate results.
  • Unless required for temporary processing, security, troubleshooting, or legal reasons, we do not intend to retain such inputs longer than necessary.
